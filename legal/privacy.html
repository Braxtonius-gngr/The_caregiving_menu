Privacy policy

Draft of November 25, 2026. Also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy and Terms of use.

The short version

  • The Caregiving Menu helps a family keep one shared record for someone they care for: medicines, readings, meals, fluids, visits, documents and notes.
  • That record is health information. We use it only to run the app for your family.
  • We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never share it with advertisers or data brokers. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your family decides who is in it. You can download everything, leave, or delete your family at any time from Settings → Your data.

Who we are

The Caregiving Menu is run by [COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us"). Contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL].

What we collect

About you (the account holder)

  • Your email address, and your name and email from Google if you sign in with Google.
  • The name you choose to show to your family, and your role in each family (owner, caregiver or viewer).
  • Your consent choices (for example, that you saw the AI notice), with the date.

About the person you care for (entered by your family)

  • Medicines, doses, the times they are given and whether each dose was given.
  • Readings such as blood pressure, pulse, blood sugar, oxygen, temperature and weight; fluids in and out; meals and diet rules.
  • Conditions, allergies, lab results, visit notes, questions for the care team, and contacts for the care team and pharmacy.
  • Photos and documents you add, such as pill bottles, hospital papers or insurance cards. These can show names, dates of birth and ID numbers.
  • Notes from a visit if you use Scribe. Scribe turns speech into text; see "Voice" below.

Created by the app

  • An edit history: who added or changed each entry, and when.
  • Records of exports, deletion requests and people leaving the family.
  • Reports you send with "This was wrong?" or "Something's wrong?". A report includes your words, what the AI was given and said, the photo it read, the app version and your phone or browser type.
  • A count of AI use per family (which feature and its cost), so we can keep within each family's allowance.

What we do not collect

No advertising IDs, no location tracking, no contacts from your phone, and no analytics or tracking cookies. On your device, the app keeps your sign-in and a copy of the family's data so it works offline. That copy stays in the app's own storage and is not shared with other sites.

How we use it

  • To show and sync your family's shared record on every member's phone.
  • To run the features you choose: reading a photo, turning speech into entries, answering questions about the record, and suggesting questions for the pharmacist.
  • To keep the service safe and working: sign-in, bot checks, fixing errors you report, and limiting AI cost.
  • To meet legal duties, such as answering a valid legal request.

We do not use your family's health information to train AI models, and we do not let our service providers do so.

AI features

Snap it, Fill from a photo, Say it, Scribe, Ask her file, Catch me up, Pharmacist questions and the kitchen helpers send what you give them (photos, text or a transcript, plus the parts of the record the feature needs) to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce an answer. The answer is shown to you to check before anything is saved. The first time you use an AI feature, the app asks for your agreement, and you can always type things in yourself instead.

Anthropic processes this for us under its commercial terms. It does not use it to train its models, and it keeps it only as its data retention policy describes. Voice

Say it, Scribe and voice questions use your phone's or browser's own speech-to-text (for example, Google in Chrome or Apple on iPhone). That service hears the audio under its own privacy terms. We receive only the resulting text, never the audio. Before a Scribe session, everyone present must agree to it.

Who can see your family's information

  • Your family. Owners and caregivers can read and add; viewers can only read. An owner approves every new member.
  • Us, only when needed. We look at a family's information only to answer a report or request you send us, to fix a problem you ask us to fix, or when the law requires it. Reports you send are visible to us and to your family's owners.
  • Our service providers, which process it only to run the app for us:
    • Supabase: stores the data and photos and handles sign-in (servers in the United States).
    • Anthropic: AI features (see above).
    • Twilio: checks phone numbers you save (it receives only the number).
    • Cloudflare: checks that a person, not a bot, is signing in.
    • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google", for sign-in.
    • GitHub: hosts the app's pages (it sees ordinary web traffic, no health information).
  • Public lookups. To find a medicine label or nutrition facts, the app sends only a medicine or food name, or a barcode, to the U.S. FDA (openFDA), the USDA or Open Food Facts. It never sends a person's name.
  • Legal requests. If we receive a valid legal order, we share only what it requires and tell you unless the law forbids it.

We will never sell health information or share it for advertising. If [COMPANY NAME] is ever sold or merged, this promise goes with the data, and we will tell you first.

How long we keep it

  • Your family's record is kept while the family exists.
  • Deleted entries go to the family's Deleted list, where they can be restored for 30 days. Photos in deleted entries are removed after 30 days. [OPEN: deleted entries themselves currently stay in the Deleted list until the family is deleted; planned change to erase them after 30 days.]
  • Deleting the family (Settings → Your data) locks it at once and erases everything (records, photos, edit history, reports and the sign-ins of members who are in no other family) after 7 days. Any owner can cancel within those 7 days. Daily backups are kept for 7 days, so erased data is gone from backups within another 7 days.
  • We keep a one-line receipt that a family was erased, with no health information, as proof.
  • When someone leaves, what they entered stays with the family, because it is part of the cared-for person's record. They choose whether their name stays on it or is replaced with "Former member" for good.

Your choices and rights

  • Get a copy: Settings → Your data → Export. Owners can export the whole family; anyone can export their own data.
  • Correct: edit any entry; the edit history keeps the old version.
  • Delete: leave the family, delete your account, or (owners) delete the family.
  • Withdraw consent to AI features: stop using them and type entries yourself; ask us to delete past AI reports.
  • You can also email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We answer within 45 days and never charge for it. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; we answer appeals within 45 days.

The person you care for

The person you care for may not use the app. When you add them, the app asks whether they agreed, whether you are their legal representative (for example, health care power of attorney or guardian), or neither yet. They, or their legal representative, can ask us at [PRIVACY EMAIL] to see, correct or delete their information. [LAWYER: confirm how to verify these requests.]

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our storage provider. Each family's information is walled off from every other family by rules in the database itself, which we test automatically. Sensitive actions, such as exporting the whole family or deleting it, need a fresh sign-in. If health information is ever exposed without permission, we will tell affected families, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission and anyone else the law requires, within the legal deadlines (at most 60 days).

Children

Accounts are for people 18 or older. The person cared for may be any age, and their information is entered by an adult in the family.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. We will never use health information in a new way without asking first.
