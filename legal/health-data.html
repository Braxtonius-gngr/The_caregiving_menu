Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Draft of November 25, 2026. This policy is written for Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws in Nevada and Connecticut, and we apply it to everyone. Our general Privacy policy covers the rest.

Who we are

[COMPANY NAME], [POSTAL ADDRESS]. Contact for anything in this policy: [PRIVACY EMAIL].

1. Consumer health data we collect

  • Medicines: names, strengths, doses, when they are given, whether each dose was given, label text, pharmacy and prescriber.
  • Measurements: blood pressure, pulse and heart rhythm symbols, blood sugar, oxygen, temperature, weight, fluids in and out (including urine output and its appearance).
  • Conditions, allergies, lab results and diet rules set by the care team.
  • Meals and food eaten, with nutrition estimates.
  • Visits: notes and transcripts of care visits (Scribe), questions for clinicians and their answers, and to-dos.
  • Photos and documents of pill bottles, hospital papers, lab reports, insurance cards and screens of home monitors.
  • Exercise and therapy logs.
  • Inferences: AI summaries of the record, suggested questions for the pharmacist, and comparisons of dose times with label instructions. These are records, not diagnoses.
  • Data about the account holder that could reveal health information about the person they care for, such as membership in a caregiving family.

2. Why we collect it

  • To keep and sync the shared record your family asked for, and show it to the members your family approved.
  • To run the features you choose to use (reading photos, turning speech into entries, answering questions about the record, suggesting questions for the pharmacist).
  • To keep the service secure, answer reports and requests, and meet legal duties.

We collect and share health data only as needed for the service you asked for. For anything beyond that, such as the optional AI features, we ask for your consent first, and you can withdraw it at any time.

3. Where it comes from

  • Directly from you and the members of your family (typed, spoken, photographed or uploaded).
  • From the app, which creates edit history, AI results you ask for, and dose-time comparisons.
  • From public reference sources (U.S. FDA labels, USDA and Open Food Facts nutrition data) when you look up a medicine or food. We send them only the medicine name, food name or barcode.

5. Your rights and how to use them

  • Know and access: confirm whether we hold your health data, get a copy, and get the list of every third party and affiliate we shared it with (above). In the app: Settings → Your data → Export. The export includes this list.
  • Delete: in the app, leave the family, delete your account, or (owners) delete the family. We also delete it from backups (within 7 days of erasure) and tell our processors to delete it.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using AI features at any time; email us to withdraw consent to anything else.
  • By email: write to [PRIVACY EMAIL] from the email on your account (or tell us how to verify you). We answer within 45 days, which we may extend once by 45 days if needed, telling you why. Requests are free, up to twice a year.
  • Appeal: if we refuse a request, reply to our answer to appeal. We answer within 45 days and explain why. If you still disagree, you can contact your state Attorney General (for Washington: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint).

The person cared for, or their legal representative, can use these rights too, even without an account. [LAWYER: confirm verification of a representative's authority.]

6. Changes

We will tell you in the app before any change that matters, and ask for consent again before using health data in a new way.
